# Consistency Plan — ClaudeNifskope Workspace Manager Docks

Anchors spot-checked against `E:\Projects\ClaudeNifskope` before writing: `wwBoxedButtonQss` is `static` at `src/nifskope_ui.cpp:418` with no header declaration; `heading()` at `src/posetools.cpp:56-62` is the only factored version; the seven `palette(mid)` labels are exactly where the audit says (5 in `riggingtools.cpp` + 1 vertex paint + 2 in `collisiontools.cpp`); `QColor(228,228,232)` appears 14× in `nifskope_ui.cpp` and 3× in `timeline.cpp`; `dTimeline` is added at `nifskope_ui.cpp:8003` and never hidden; `advancedWidgets` at `riggingtools.cpp:4746` really does omit `createSkin` and `mappingRow`; `skeletontools.cpp:565` really does use `wwSkinColor("danger")` for secondary selection; `insertKeyAtTime` really does `continue` on `QuatVal` at `timelineedit.cpp:534`; `poseBoneAt` really does accept `skeletonView` at `glview.cpp:1574`; `applyPhysics` at `collisiontools.cpp:1361` really is bare `nif->set<>`.

## 1. The house style, as it actually exists

There is a real house style. It is just never in one place, and no panel implements all of it. Pick these as the reference implementations:

**Colour sourcing — Skeleton Manager (`src/skeletontools.cpp`).** Eleven `wwSkinColor` calls, zero hex literals outside a comment. It proves the rule stated in the source itself at `nifskope_ui.cpp:354` ("add a colour here rather than writing a literal anywhere else") is achievable in a panel of real complexity. Standardise on it — with the one correction that its secondary-selection colour is wrong (see below).

**Section headings — Pose Manager's `heading()` (`src/posetools.cpp:56-62`).** A bare `QLabel { font-weight: 600; }`, no ad-hoc padding, factored into a function. Its own comment says "matching the other manager docks" while it sits in an anonymous namespace nobody else can reach, which is precisely why three other idioms exist.

**Collapsible sections — Collision Manager (`src/spells/collisiontools.cpp:2374-2392`).** `QToolButton` with `setArrowType(RightArrow/DownArrow)` over a body widget, state persisted to `CollisionManager/AdvancedExpanded`. Also done a second time for the creation defaults at `:2206-2225`, and copied correctly by `PhysicsSimPanel` (`physicspanel.cpp:555-567`).

**Status line and empty state — Pose Manager (`src/posetools.cpp:123`).** One word-wrapped `QLabel` immediately under the heading that carries the empty-state sentence at construction and is then rewritten by every operation (`:304, 432, 613, 639, 677, 697`). Sentence-shaped empty states with a full stop are near-universal already; Materials is the only main view without one.

**Undo — `nifSnapshotOp` (`src/nifsnapshot.h:69-98`), as used by Skeleton, Rigging, Vertex Paint and Materials' non-bulk paths.** ~89 call sites across 18 files. Collision's structural ops use it correctly; its live editors do not, and the Animation dock copies it instead of calling it.

**Model following and selection sync — Collision Manager (`collisiontools.cpp:2618-2645`).** Subscribes to `modelReset`, `rowsInserted`, `rowsRemoved`, `dataChanged` and `linksChanged`; routes `currentNifIndexChanged` through `selectRowForBlock()` (`:1162-1188`) which matches, selects *and* scrolls; and guards the handler with `!dock->isVisible()` (`:2641`).

**Dock lifecycle — Skeleton Manager (`skeletontools.cpp:341` + `1079-1080`).** `setAllowedAreas(Left|Right)`, then `addDockWidget`, then `hide()`.

**Button policy stated once — Animation Manager's `mkBtn`/`mkIconBtn` (`src/ui/widgets/timeline.cpp:721-736`).** One factory lambda, so every transport button gets the same `setAutoRaise(true)`. Contrast UV, where the policy is decided per button.

**Boxed menu buttons — `wwBoxedButtonQss` (`nifskope_ui.cpp:418`).** Transparent background, transparent border, `bgBtnDown` checked plate. The rationale comment above it (`:405-417`) is explicit that bordered boxes were deliberately removed. This is the intended look; the problem is purely that it is unreachable outside its translation unit.

The single worst offender is `src/spells/riggingtools.cpp`, which builds two of the eight docks (Rigging and Vertex Paint), does not include `wwskin.h`, and calls `wwSkinColor` zero times.

## 2. Inconsistencies worth fixing, ranked by confusion ÷ cost

**1. Skeleton's selection handler runs, and rewrites global selection, while its dock is hidden.** `skeletontools.cpp:988-1010` has no `dock->isVisible()` guard, and when the incoming block is a `bhkNiCollisionObject`/`bhkNPCollisionObject` it calls `skope->select()` again on the Target node. So in *every* workspace — Collision included — clicking a collision body silently jumps to its owner bone, fighting the Collision Manager whose own rows are collision objects. The same handler runs a full `skeletonAnalyse()` (walks every vertex of every skinned shape, `:188-236`) on every block-list click app-wide. Fix: add the `!dock->isVisible()` early-out that `collisiontools.cpp:2641` already has, above the redirect. One line, and it fixes a cross-workspace behaviour nobody could explain.

**2. Collision's live physics editors write the model with no undo entry.** `applyPhysics()` (`collisiontools.cpp:1361-1411`) writes ~20 fields with bare `nif->set<>`; `applyLayerSelection():1338` and `applyMaterialSelection():1359` the same. `NifModel::set` never pushes a command on its own. The same panel snapshots Compile / Import Donor / Apply Safe Fixes correctly (`:1445, 1553, 1683, 1782`), so undo *appears* to work and then reverts something else. Fix: wrap the three `apply*` in `nifSnapshotOp`, triggered on `editingFinished`/`activated` rather than `valueChanged` so a `CollisionDragSpinBox` scrub is one entry, not one per pixel.

**3. Rigging's collapsed "Advanced steps" box still shows two of its children.** `advancedWidgets` at `riggingtools.cpp:4746` is `{ hint, rebind, generate, importNodes, bindBones, transferWeights }` — `createSkin` (added `:4739`) and the `mappingRow` layout (added `:4745`) are missing, and the group defaults unchecked (`:4692`). Every fresh install shows a collapsed box containing "0. Create Skin (bind to node)…" and the Mapping / Max Bones row floating in it. Fix: add `createSkin` to the list and wrap `mappingRow` in a `QWidget` so it can be hidden. Two lines. (The structural fix is item 12.)

**4. Muted hint text has two sources and the commoner one is not theme-controlled at all.** `wwSkinColor("textMuted")` in Pose (`posetools.cpp:231`), Skeleton (`423, 481`), PhysicsSimPanel (`physicspanel.cpp:689`), Collision (`collisiontools.cpp:1847`); `color: palette(mid)` in Rigging (`riggingtools.cpp:4521, 4535, 4592, 4627, 4737`), Vertex Paint (`riggingtools.cpp:6458`) and Collision again (`collisiontools.cpp:1107, 2425`). `loadTheme()` (`nifskope_ui.cpp:11878-11913`) sets Window, WindowText, Base, AlternateBase, ToolTip*, Text, Button, ButtonText, BrightText, Link, Highlight, HighlightedText — and never Mid. So `palette(mid)` resolves to the OS default and follows neither the app theme nor the user's custom colours. Collision shows both greys in one column, four rows apart. Fix: replace all seven with `wwSkinColor("textMuted")`; optionally also set `QPalette::Mid/Midlight/Dark` in `loadTheme()` so stragglers are at least theme-aware.

**5. Four renditions of "which row is selected", one of which is the app's error colour.** The Block List convention (`bg #4a7ab0` active / `#2b425f` secondary, text `#FF9D00`/`#FF7200`) is hardcoded identically in six files: `model/nifmodel.cpp:1288-1291`, `nifskope.cpp:261-264`, `collisiontools.cpp:2057-2058`, `meshtools.cpp:983-984` and `1124-1125`, `riggingtools.cpp:79-81`. Pose uses a third set (`posetools.cpp:401-402`), and its blue has already drifted to `#2b3b5c` against everyone's `#2b425f` while the comment says it is copying the Block List. UV and Timeline have no selection styling at all. Fix: add `selBgActive/selBgInactive/selTextActive/selTextInactive` to `skinVars` (`nifskope_ui.cpp:361-386`) with light-theme columns, export a `wwSelectionTreeQss()` next to `wwSkinColor` in `wwskin.h`, and call it from all seven sites. Delete the danger-red at `skeletontools.cpp:565`.

**6. Every drawn icon in the app is invisible in the Light theme.** `QColor(228,228,232)` is the glyph colour at 16 of 17 `tlMakeIcon` call sites (14 in `nifskope_ui.cpp`, 3 in `timeline.cpp`); only `nifskope_ui.cpp:10193-10194` sources it from the skin. In Light, `wwSkinColor("text")` is `#202020` while these stay `#e4e4e8` on an `#e9e9e9` plate — viewport-mode selector, gizmo/orient/pivot/snap menus, X-ray, wireframe, shading, magnet, eye, nodes, bulb, workspace and panel selectors, and the whole Animation transport row. **7. Materials' bulk path edits: one whole-model snapshot per row, no confirmation, and a count that lies.** Replace All (`meshtools.cpp:1495-1513`) and Retarget Folder (`:1519-1543`) call `setText(2,…)` per item; each fires `itemChanged` (`:1364`) which calls `nifSnapshotOp` (`:1381`), which serialises the entire NIF twice. 200 paths = ~400 full serialisations, 200 undo entries, each retaining two copies of the file, and no way to undo as a unit. Fix: set the existing `*applying` guard (`:1365`) around the loop, write inside one `nifSnapshotOp`, add a match-count confirmation, and clear `ItemIsEditable`/`ItemIsDropEnabled` on those rows at `:1345` so every write path refuses them uniformly.

**8. The dock's timer also always wraps regardless of `aAnimLoop`, ignores the quick panel's Speed/Reverse, and `btnPlay`'s checked state never tracks `aAnimPlay`. Fix: route `transportToggle()` through the connected `playPauseRequested`, read scene `animSpeed` for the per-tick delta, honour `aAnimLoop` at the wrap, and mirror `aAnimPlay` onto `btnPlay` the way `addAnimActions` already does for Loop and Switch.

**9. ## 3. Feature gaps worth closing

**Real, and cheap.**

*Vertex Paint has no Fill Selection.* All three paint modes let you build an element selection that acts as a paint mask (`glview.cpp:18524`); Weight and Segment Paint let you flood it (`fillRiggingWeightSelection`/`fillSegmentPaintSelection`, `glview.cpp:2074, 2109`; keys at `:20312-20321`; RMB menu at `:16983-17002`). The shortcut is registered as "Paint: Fill Selection (weight / segment)" (`glview.cpp:225`), so the omission is baked into the user-facing string. The implementation is a near-copy with a colour instead of a weight, and the synthetic-stroke plumbing exists.

*Skeleton's bones are drawn and inert.* The workspace draws octahedral bones with forced-on name labels (`glview.cpp:1835-1841, 4138-4141`) that look exactly like Pose Mode's clickable bones. `GLView::poseBoneAt` explicitly accepts `skeletonView` (`glview.cpp:1574` — verified), but the hover handler (`:20840`) and click handler (`:21440-21453`) both gate on `poseMode` alone. Widen two guards and route `poseBonePicked` to the tree; the Skeleton tree currently has no viewport→tree path at all.

*Rotation keys cannot be inserted from the Animation dock.* `insertKeyAtTime` skips quaternion channels outright (`timelineedit.cpp:534` — verified), so I or double-click on a `NiTransformInterpolator` rotation lane produces no key and no message. `writeChannelKeys` handles `QuatVal` fine (`:361, 404`) and the auto-key path writes quat keys through it (`:1089`). Rotation is the most-keyed channel there is. This is a bug wearing a gap's clothes.

*Collision has no filter over the widest tree in the app.* Animation, Materials, Rigging, Pose and Skeleton all open with one; Collision's 7-column inventory lists every compiled and editable body with shapes as children — dozens of rows on a full-body ragdoll — with no way to narrow them. A `QLineEdit` and a pass over `topLevelItem()`.

*Nothing persists in Vertex Paint or Pose.* Animation persists nine keys, Collision a dozen, UV seven. Vertex Paint stores neither radius, strength, channel nor brush colour, so every session starts at white / 25% / 32 px; Rigging persists only `Rigging/MappingMode` and `Rigging/MaxInfluences` (`riggingtools.cpp:4724-4733`) and resets brush mode, weight, strength, radius, Accumulate, Mirror and the heatmap toggle. UV's own α (`uvtools.cpp:5610`) and `showStretch` don't persist while all seven of their neighbours do.

**Real, medium cost.**

*Vertex Paint has one brush behaviour.* The weight section in the same file offers Add/Subtract/Replace/Smooth plus Accumulate and Mirror (`riggingtools.cpp:4543-4588`); Vertex Paint always accumulates (`:6606-6609`), with no smooth, no erase-to-white, no mirror. Same file, same brush engine, same edit cage, same toolbar Brush toggle. Smooth is the most-asked-for vertex-colour brush and the mirror machinery already exists (`:5127-5167`).

*Findings that can't be reached.* Animation's lint is a dialog you can double-click to select the offending block, with a pick-a-node fix (`timelineedit.cpp:1563-1611`); Collision's has Apply Safe Fixes (`collisiontools.cpp:1766-1794`). Skeleton reports "dangling skin bone" and "duplicate node name" as a static red `QLabel` (`skeletontools.cpp:536-545`) with no block number, in the panel whose whole job is repairing rigs. Materials' Check Materials is a plain information box (`meshtools.cpp:1545-1559`). Both should be `QListWidget`s with a double-click `select()` each panel already makes elsewhere.

*Copy/paste a physics body's setup.* A body has ~20 tunables; matching a second to a first means retyping all of them. Collision's context menu offers only "Copy Material Name and Value" (`collisiontools.cpp:2461`). Materials has three granularities of this (`meshtools.cpp:1655-1675`) and Animation has channel copy/paste. One `QVariantMap` clipboard plus a paste inside one `nifSnapshotOp` — and it substitutes for multi-select, which is the more invasive change.

*UV and Animation keys bypass the rebindable registry.* All 53 registered ids are `viewport.*`; `uvtools.cpp:5219-5300` hardcodes ~18 keys and `timeline.cpp:2179-2258` ~9, neither calling `shortcuts.matches()`. UV deliberately mirrors the viewport's Blender verbs (G/R/S, A, Alt+A, Ctrl+I, L, Ctrl+L, 1-4) against a viewport where those same verbs *are* rebindable, so rebinding one half desyncs the pair, and neither panel's keys appear in the keyboard settings.

**Not really gaps — say so plainly.**

*Materials multi-select.* Replace All, Retarget Folder and Assign to Selected Shapes already give it bulk paths; a single-selection tree with a working preview is the right shape. Collision multi-select is the one that matters, and copy/paste covers most of it more cheaply.

*Isolate in Rigging and Pose.* Paint modes already scope Edit Mode to the target shape and Pose has a bone filter. Materials is the one case with a real argument — it already knows every shape using a material (`meshtools.cpp:1777-1784`) and `Scene::soloNode` already has a working consumer (`glnode.cpp:444`) and an existing caller in the Timeline — but rank it below everything above.

*UV's typographic toolbar.* Not an icon gap. A dense Blender-style header of ⇄ / ▾ / α / … is defensible and internally consistent; it just needs the two menu buttons framed like everyone else's.

*Collapsible sections everywhere.* Only Rigging is broken and only Pose is too tall. Pose needs a `QScrollArea`, not folding. Add the shared helper because Rigging needs fixing anyway; don't retrofit folds into Materials, Skeleton or UV on principle.

*Merging a skeleton in from the Skeleton Manager.* Reuse from Pose's Load skeleton… is genuinely near-total, and the duplicate-name warning would land in the panel that already reports duplicate node names. But nobody has asked, and the Pose use case (pose several pieces as one rig) is not the Skeleton use case (inspect and repair). Defensible either way; do it last or not at all.

*A UV test harness.* Developer-facing, and it can't be written until UV names more than its three widgets (`UVManagerDock`, `UVEditorStatus`, `UVOperatorPanel`) across a 5,941-line file. Real, but it ranks below every user-visible item here.

## 4. What to leave alone

**UV's bespoke `UVEditCommand` instead of `nifSnapshotOp`** (`uvtools.cpp:325-377`, pushed from `commitTransformUndo:4863`). The deviation is documented at `:318-324`: a whole-model snapshot reloads the file and blanks the editor mid-gesture. It correctly falls back to `nifSnapshotOp` for the two structural operators, Rip (`:4072`) and Smart UV Project (`:4289`). This is the right call, not a drift.

**The Animation Manager living in the bottom dock area.** It is a timeline; a 4,000-frame ruler in a 300px right column is not an improvement. Make the placement *explicit* with `setAllowedAreas(Bottom|Left|Right)` rather than removing the special case.

**Materials enumerating the whole file rather than the selection.** It is the one panel whose job is a file-wide inventory, and Replace All / Retarget / Find Duplicates depend on that scope.

**The GL and data-encoding colour tables.** `uvtools.cpp:228-266` (canvas palette), `timeline_p.h:31-38` (`tlCompColor` X/Y/Z/W curve colours) and the weight heatmap ramp encode *meaning*, not chrome. Routing them through `skinVars` buys nothing and costs legibility. (The Timeline's playhead red `0xe04040`, out-of-range red `0xe24b4a` and marker amber `0xd8a030` are borderline — leave them until someone reports a Light-theme problem.)

**The Timeline painting from `QPalette` rather than `wwSkinColor`.** `QPalette` *does* follow `loadTheme()`. Only the hardcoded icon colour is broken, and that is item 6 above, app-wide.

**Vertex Paint and Rigging sharing `riggingtools.cpp`.** File organisation, invisible to users, and the two docks genuinely share the brush/stroke/status plumbing. Splitting it is churn.

**Pose and Skeleton showing the same rig in different registers** — a flat pickable list for posing, a 4-column hierarchy tree with shape/vert/weight numbers for inspection. Different tasks, correctly different presentations.

**Rigging and Vertex Paint being FO4-only** (`riggingIsSkinnedShape`, `:164-178`). Documented, deliberate, and not a consistency problem.

**UV's tighter toolbar spacing.** Its dense two-row header earns a tighter bar; only the *root* layout should match the 6,6,6,6 / spacing-5 majority so the left edge lines up across workspaces.
