# Points are cell units (x east, y north; a texel is 1/32 cell) of a texel INSIDE the body on the bake they were
# taken from (10-28); the baker resolves each by position and reports an id that moved.
#   body <id> <x> <y> <class> [dir <fx> <fy> <tx> <ty>]   class: sea | river | lake | swamp | calm
#        lake / swamp / calm = no current; river = solved per texel (dir: the direction from -> to, when the
#        automatic rule gets it wrong); a body with no line keeps the automatic rule.
#   join <dam|tributary> <x> <y> into <x> <y>   the body at the first point drains into the body at the second
#   channels box <x0> <y0> <x1> <y1> radius <tx> lmin <tx> ocean <x> <y> class <river|sea> speed <u/s>
#        inside the box, sea water narrower than ~2 x radius texels (opening at that radius; the box edge counts
#        as sea) that is not the ocean (the opened part holding the ocean point) splits from the sea body: one new
#        body per connected part whose farthest texel is >= lmin texels from the ocean; each is solved with the
#        ocean contact held at 0 and a source on every texel plus the bodies that drain into it (dams, joins).
