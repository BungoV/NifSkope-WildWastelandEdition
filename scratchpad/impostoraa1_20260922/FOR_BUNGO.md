# Impostors: the torn paper and the 2x bake

**1. The torn paper is caused by how the card is DRAWN, not how it is baked.** `pics/tear_blast_n4.png`

- The sheet holds the whole trunk. One frame on its own has it at 97% coverage.
- But the drawer averages three frames taken from far-apart angles: one from the side, one from 63 degrees up, and a side neighbour.
- The three put the trunk in different places. The average drops below the cut, so that piece of the card vanishes.
- The better bake cannot fix this. Three fixes are ranked for the director.

**2. The 2x bake itself: cleaner edges, crevices back.** `pics/maple_n4_edge3x.png` (mesh | before | after, zoomed 3x)

- The dark edge halos are gone.
- The maple is less blobby: 0.49 to 0.57 match at the bake angles.
- The rock's crevice contrast went from 0.54 to 0.68 of the mesh, the most this resolution allows.
