C4 (redone) the notch, against an INJECTED repeat of known amplitude.
   A repeat is coherent and axis-aligned, so it would inflate any
   orientation statistic.  Injected into a real vanilla sheet at
   amplitudes spanning TILING2`s whole range (its ceiling is 0.264 and
   the rung reads about 2.0), the NOTCHED reading must not move.
   amp (8-bit)  un-notched     notched
   0.0             0.1714      0.1712
   0.5             0.1714      0.1712
   2.0             0.1709      0.1712
   8.0             0.1722      0.1712
   the notched reading drifts 0.0000 over a 16x range of injected repeat
   (the un-notched one moves 0.0008).  The notch does separate the grid from the swirl.
   (s1c`s C4 used the `average` bake as the no-repeat control and read
   it ABOVE the rung, 0.28..0.70 against 0.15..0.32.  That bake replaces
   every land texture by one flat colour, so its fine band is only
   alpha-blend edges and VCLR ramps -- highly oriented and almost
   energy-free.  It is a valid no-repeat control for a PERIODICITY, which
   is what TILING3 used it for, and a useless one for an ORIENTATION.)
