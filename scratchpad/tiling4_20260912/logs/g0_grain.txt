WHY.  Vanilla`s own grain across these seven sheets runs 1.37 to 6.93 on
hp SD (a factor of 5.1) and 2.0 to 46.8 on local variance (a factor of
23.8), because it is set by what terrain is there.  Our composite`s runs
2.88 to 3.73 and 7.2 to 16.0 -- it is set by which land textures are
painted, and a change to the SAMPLER cannot move one sheet`s grain to
its own chunk`s vanilla value.  The gate as written is not a gate on the
sampler.
