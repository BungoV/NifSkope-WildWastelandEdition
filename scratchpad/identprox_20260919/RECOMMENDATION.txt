WHERE IT STARTS JOINING SEPARATE HOUSES
---------------------------------------
With the MESH measure: 128 u is the last safe gap.  At 256 u an identity first
holds placements of TWO different reference buildings.  The measure is what
protects you, not the gap:
  aabb 16 u  ALREADY welds 286 placements across 9 Creation Kit layers
             (4 South Boston city blocks + the highway), an 18,121 u identity
  obb  16 u  the same weld, 173 placements, 8 layers, 18,121 u
  mesh 16..128 u   the ONLY identity over the 6,110 u threshold is the highway
             itself (Roads_FlyingHighway, 15,913..16,280 u) -- which is what a
             highway is -- plus three RockCliff01 placements
So: use the mesh measure.  A box gap of any kind bridges a street because the
elevated deck's box hangs over the buildings underneath it.

WELDING THE RUN MAKES THAT WORSE, NOT BETTER.  Under kit/aabb/16 (today's rule
with the road kit added to the eligible list) the 10 deck pieces become ONE

shares the deck's identity can no longer be shadowed by it.  It also drags in
22 non-highway placements, among them the 3-placement Theater_Buildings layer.
