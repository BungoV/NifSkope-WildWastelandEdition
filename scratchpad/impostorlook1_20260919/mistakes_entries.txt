## 2026-09-19 -- "the disagreement is the SHEET's", published without ever putting the sheet's own frame beside the draw

Lane IMPOSTORFIX5 measured the fine-twig maple's card against its mesh at the
sixteen directions its own bake used, found IoU 0.4673 at blend 0 and 0.4673 at
blend 1, and reasoned: parallax is a provable no-op at a bake direction, the two
numbers are identical, therefore the view ray is right and what is left is the
SHEET's. The reasoning is sound and the conclusion does not follow. It rules out
the parallax step; it says nothing about what happens to the sheet's value after
it is read.

Lane IMPOSTORLOOK1 drew the third picture nobody had drawn: the raw frame cut
out of `0004a074_oct_d.DDS` and composited with its coverage as a fraction, in
the same cell as the mesh grab and the card grab
(`images/A_maple_n4_every_bake_angle.png`). The baked frame is a recognisable
thin twiggy tree. The card is blobs. The detail is IN the sheet and the draw
throws it away: `res/shaders/impostor_oct.frag` tests `colour.a < alphaThreshold`
and then writes `vec4( ..., 1.0 )`, so a texel with 7 per cent coverage draws
fully opaque, and 94 per cent of that subject's silhouette is partial coverage.

How it was found: by making the picture the brief asked for, not by a new
measurement. THE RULE: a claim of the form "the defect is in stage X" ships with
stage X's OWN output beside the final one. Two numbers that agree with each
other rule out what sits between them and nothing else.

## 2026-09-19 -- the brief named the fallback filename and the lane used the refused one first

IMPOSTORLOOK1's brief says, in its header, `report.md` incremental, "fallback
name `DELIVERABLE_TEXT.md`". The harness refuses to let an agent write
`report.md`; lane IMPOSTORFIX5 hit the same refusal the same day and wrote it
into its own report. This lane wrote `report.md` anyway, was refused, and then
used the fallback -- one wasted tool call and a paragraph of explanation that a
reader of the brief did not need.

How it was found: the tool refused. THE RULE: when a brief names a fallback for
a path, the fallback is not a recovery route, it is the instruction. Read the
header's parenthetical before the first write, not after it.
