A CAVEAT THE NUMBERS THEMSELVES FORCE, and it is not a small one.
On both of these sheets the reading sits BELOW the instrument`s own
12-period null sweep -- vanilla`s included (0.201 against a null of
0.449). A null sweep`s maximum over twelve wrong periods is a
significance floor for ONE sheet in isolation, and at 512 texels on a
single chunk it is simply too coarse to separate these readings. The
threshold that IS usable is the one a6_pick.py froze on the 22 shipped
vanilla sheets -- absolute 0.264, the worst of them -- and that is what
the verdicts above are graded against. Stated here rather than left for
someone to find in the table.

WHAT THE SHIPPED DEFAULT DOES AND DOES NOT DO. `ship` is the ruling:
vanilla`s `_msn` byte for byte and the crevice term on our colour. It
does NOT touch the repeat -- the warp is OFF by default, because the
7-sheet selection in a6_pick.py did NOT meet its own gate (6 of 7) and
this lane refuses to ship a proposal as a pass. `stoch` is that
proposal, one flag away, and its two numbers are printed above so
bungo can decide on the numbers rather than on a recommendation.
