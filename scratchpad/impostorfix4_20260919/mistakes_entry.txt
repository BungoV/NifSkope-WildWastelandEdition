## 2026-09-19 -- IMPOSTORFIX4 -- measured "compression error" against a file the compressor never saw

What was done: to decide whether the 4x4 chips at the trunk were BC1 endpoint
error, the lane re-encoded `<id>_oct_normal.png` -- the PNG that sits beside the
`_n` DDS in the bake output -- and compared the result with the shipped DDS. It
reported a mean height error of 5.06 to 7.49 levels, a 95th percentile of 18.32
to 36.52, and worst blocks of 69 to 206, and it was about to name the encoder's
min/max-LUMINANCE endpoint rule as the defect on that evidence.

What was true: that PNG is the BAKE's output, not the ENCODER's input. Between
the two, `src/lodgen.cpp:3079-3088` runs `lodgenDilateFrames` three times and
then `lodgenRepairOctHeight`, and the comment at 3102 says the sheets are
"converted once from the bake's PNGs" -- converted FROM, after the tail has run.
Most of what was being called compression error was the dilate and the height
repair doing exactly their job.

How it was found: the known-answer control the lane had written for its own
model of `lodgenEncodeBC1Block` FAILED -- it reproduced 0.88 to 17.38 per cent
of the shipped endpoint bytes. A model that reproduces one byte in a hundred is
not a model with a bug in it; it is a model of a different input. Rebuilding the
input by re-running the source's own tail took the control to 100.00 per cent of
endpoints and 99.90 to 99.98 per cent of indices on all five subjects, and on
THAT input the endpoint rule costs 0.04 to 0.12 levels -- refuted, not convicted.

The rule: an intermediate file in a pipeline's output folder is not the next
stage's input until the source says it is. Before measuring stage N's error,
find the line that CALLS stage N and read what it is handed. And when a
known-answer control fails by an order of magnitude rather than a little, the
first hypothesis is that the wrong thing is being measured, not that the model
is slightly wrong.

## 2026-09-19 -- IMPOSTORFIX4 -- a refuter that could not have fired

What was done: to test whether the colour dilate bleeds coverage outward, the
lane counted `_d` alpha values strictly between 0 and 16 outside the silhouette,
found none on any of the five subjects, and wrote that down as the measurement
proving the stage contributes zero.

What was true: `lodgenDilateFrames` fills from the frame MEAN, and a frame mean
alpha is normally at or above 16. A dilate that bled would therefore have
written values the test classified as "covered" and never counted. The test
could only have fired on a bleed of a kind the function does not perform. The
positive control chosen beside it was no better: `_n` alpha is sway, which is
mostly zero, so it had nothing to detect either.

How it was found: by asking, before quoting the number, what value a bleeding
dilate would actually write -- which is in the same function, eight lines above
the loop that was being tested.

The rule (CONSTITUTION 4): an invariant counts only when it is shown to FAIL on
the broken case. Write the broken case down explicitly -- "if it bled it would
write X" -- and check that the test sees X. The stage's verdict now rests on the
source (`isCoverage` makes `put` preserve `qAlpha`, src/lodgen.cpp:2494-2500,
called as `lodgenDilateFrames( alb, alb, ... )` at 3083), with the count
demoted to corroboration and its blind spot stated in the report.

## 2026-09-19 -- IMPOSTORFIX4 -- a texel page whose headline contradicted its own crop

What was done: the first `ww-texel-picture` page for the chips carried the
headline "the chip is a 4x4 block whose own height range is large, not a
silhouette edge" -- the sheet-wide finding -- above a crop chosen, correctly, as
the sheet's single worst block. On rock_n4 that worst block sits ON the
silhouette, so the page's picture argued against the page's sentence.

How it was found: by opening the PNG and looking at it, which is section 5 of
the skill. The script's own printout was right and said nothing about this.

The rule: when a page's headline is a DISTRIBUTION and its crop is an EXTREME,
the headline must carry the distribution's numbers for that subject and the page
must say the crop is not typical. The page now prints the subject's own
"blocks over 12 levels that straddle the silhouette, against the base rate for
any block" and a line saying the crop is the worst one, chosen by the metric.
