## The work
1. **The curve, before any code.** On BOTH tiles ((-20,24) and (-20,20)) baked at the fixed tiling and composite: for matched texels (exclude road texels and cover>0 texels for the first pass, so only bare ground blends are compared), plot vanilla luminance against ours; fit three models and report their residuals: (a) constant gain `v = k * o`; (b) gamma `v = a * o^g` (and the two-way sRGB<->linear slips as fixed candidates: `o` treated as linear written as sRGB, and the reverse); (c) position-dependent: the residual of the best global model mapped across the tile and correlated against the height field, the slope, the horizon AO plane and the VCLR -- a residual that follows AO/slope says vanilla baked a lighting term; one that follows VCLR says our VCLR strength is wrong; a flat residual says the global model is the whole story. Known-answer controls first (synthetic gamma slip and synthetic gain must be recovered to 3 decimals). Chroma: the same for saturation (vanilla is LESS saturated -- a gamma slip changes saturation in a predictable direction; a gain does not).
2. **Name the cause** with the fingerprint (section 2 of the report), and the code line if it is ours (the sRGB/linear handling in the bake) or the engine constant if it is theirs (a CK bake brightness / ambient / "LOD texture" setting -- search the Setting array with `fo4-engine-constant-from-ini-setting` ONLY if the fingerprint says position-independent gain and our code shows no slip).
